PART 1: GLOBAL SETTINGS (apply to every scene)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Format: Vertical 9:16, cinematic, 15 seconds total, 5 scenes of 3 seconds each.
Story: A burnt-out corporate man walks out of his office job in the night, drives across the country, finds a small rural town, rents an old farmhouse, learns farming from a gruff neighbor, and finds peace.
Tone: Heavy, masculine, grounded, nostalgic, ending in calm and serenity.
Look: Cinematic color grade, film grain, shallow depth of field, realistic skin, fabric and wood texture. Colors move from cold blue (corporate) to warm amber and gold (rural peace).

CHARACTER CONSISTENCY TAG (paste into EVERY scene prompt):
"same man, 30s, short dark hair, stubble, tired but determined eyes"

NEGATIVE PROMPT (use on every scene):
"text, watermark, subtitles, logos, distorted hands, extra fingers, cartoon, anime, warped faces, blurry, low quality, flickering, morphing"

SCENE 1 - THE CORPORATE EXIT (0-3s)

Night, rain-slick street outside a towering glass office building, one floor still glowing with cold fluorescent light, rows of empty cubicles visible through the windows. Same man, 30s, short dark hair, stubble, tired but determined eyes, in a rumpled white shirt and loosened navy tie, a laptop bag over one shoulder, pushes through the revolving door of the lobby. Outside, he pulls off the tie, tosses it into the bed of an old dusty pickup, and climbs in. Engine rumbles, headlights flare, city lights and neon reflections smear across the wet windshield, the glowing tower shrinking in the rearview mirror. Cold blue corporate tones shifting to warm amber, anamorphic lens flares, realistic fabric and skin texture, film grain. Cinematic vertical video, 9:16. Golden-hour sunset over a green valley farm. Same man, short dark hair, stubble, plaid shirt, sleeves rolled, dirt on his forearms, sits on the porch steps with a steaming coffee mug, eyes half closed, a calm smile. A scruffy stray dog sleeps on his worn boots. Behind him, straight rows of corn, tomatoes and sunflowers sway in the breeze, a red barn in the distance, chickens pecking near a wooden fence, two cows grazing in the pasture, a tractor parked beside a hay bale. Laundry flutters on a line, fireflies rise from the tall grass, soft mist settles over the fields. He breathes out slowly, shoulders relaxed, no phone in sight. Warm amber glow, serene and nostalgic tone, shallow depth of field. [Slow pull out, gentle drift]

PART 7: ASSEMBLY AND MIXING NOTES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Generate Scene 1 first, then use a frame as the image reference (image-to-video) for Scenes 2-5 to keep the face consistent through wardrobe changes.
- If a 3-second clip is not supported, generate at the minimum length and trim in editing.
- If a scene gets muddled, cut it to its single key moment (badge drop, key handoff, the nod) and keep the rest as background.
- In Scene 5, if details get dropped, remove the laundry and tractor first.
- Audio is built separately: voiceover in ElevenLabs, music in Suno/Udio, sound effects in CapCut (free libraries: Pixabay, Freesound).
- Mix levels: music -18 dB, sound effects -12 dB, voiceover -6 dB.
- Boost 100-200 Hz on the voiceover for weight and bass.
- Hard cut between Scenes 1 and 2 (engine sound carries across); crossfade audio in Scenes 3-5 so the mood softens.
- Add the title "The Quiet Life" in CapCut instead of generating text in the video.
- Turn off Hailuo native audio for full control, or keep it only as a reference layer.
